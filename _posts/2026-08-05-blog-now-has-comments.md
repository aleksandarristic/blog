This blog now has comments. I know, groundbreaking.

Disqus was out — it's an ad network cosplaying as a comment box. Utterances and giscus wanted you to sign in with GitHub, which filters out everyone except the people I argue with the least. Cusdis was perfect, except it's dead.

So: [Waline](https://waline.js.org/), self-hosted on my own box, no account required, no tracking, Gruvbox-themed so it doesn't look like a stranger crashed the page.

Scroll to the bottom of any post. Say something. Or don't — the "email me" option is finally retired either way.
